# Breakfast gets there first

NMRL's fictional station names reference prominent Kerala cities outside the
operational Kochi metro network: Thiruvananthapuram, Kozhikode, Thrissur, Kollam,
Kannur, Malappuram, Palakkad, and Alappuzha. This is a curated regional selection,
not a population ranking or a claim that these cities have no railway stations.

Research checked on 12 September 2026:

- [KMRL's project tenders](https://kochimetro.org/tender/) list DPR work for
  Thiruvananthapuram and mobility/alternative analysis for Kozhikode. Proposed
  metro projects are not presented as operating passenger services.
- [Government of India metro expansion overview](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2240324&lang=1&reg=1).
- [Kerala government urban population context](https://datahub.kerala.gov.in/)
  informs the regional selection; district populations are not city rankings.

All NMRL departures, hours, headways, platform announcements, and journey minutes
are entertainment data, not KMRL schedules. The app explicitly labels its board
as a simulation in IST. Existing saved maps retain their stored station names.

Names are deterministic from the saved visual seed and station order. Once the
base city names are used, word qualifiers form a unique sequence without numeric
suffixes. No language-model API, live traffic feed, or external timetable service
is involved.
